# Access Control

This policy establishes baseline access control requirements for all organizational information systems.

## AC-1 Access Control Policy and Procedures

- The organization SHALL develop, document, and disseminate an access control policy
- The organization SHALL review and update the access control policy at least annually

## AC-2 Account Management

- The organization SHALL manage information system accounts including establishing, activating, modifying, reviewing, disabling, and removing accounts
- The organization SHALL identify authorized users of the information system and specify access privileges
- The organization SHALL require approvals by authorized personnel for requests to create information system accounts

## AC-3 Access Enforcement

- The information system SHALL enforce approved authorizations for logical access to information and system resources
- The information system SHALL enforce approved authorizations for controlling the flow of information between interconnected systems

## IA-1 Identification and Authentication Policy

- The organization SHALL develop, document, and disseminate an identification and authentication policy
- The organization SHALL require identification and authentication of organizational users before granting access to information systems
