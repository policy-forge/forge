# Third-Party Security Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the requirements for the management of third-party services that handle confidential information for the Organization in any manner, ensuring that vendor relationships do not introduce unacceptable security risks.

## Scope

This policy applies to all computer systems and facilities of the Organization, including those managed by third parties. It applies to all employees and third parties with access to information assets.

## Policy

### Third-Party Service Providers

**Vendor Selection** — The Organization SHALL evaluate prospective third-party vendors for information security capabilities before engagement. Selection criteria SHALL include the vendor's security posture, compliance certifications, and ability to meet the Organization's data protection requirements.

**Cloud Service Provider Selection** — Cloud service providers SHALL be evaluated against the Organization's security requirements, including data residency, encryption capabilities, access controls, and incident response procedures before engagement.

**Vendor Inventory** — The Organization SHALL maintain a current inventory of all third-party vendors that have access to or process the Organization's confidential information. The inventory SHALL be reviewed periodically to ensure accuracy.

### Third-Party Security Requirements

**Risk Assessment** — A risk assessment SHALL be conducted for all third-party vendors that access, process, or store confidential information. The assessment SHALL evaluate the vendor's security controls relative to the sensitivity of data being handled.

**Risk Screening** — Third-party vendors SHOULD be screened based on the criticality of services provided and the sensitivity of data accessed. Higher-risk vendors SHALL receive more frequent and detailed security reviews.

**Information Security Responsibilities** — Third-party vendors SHALL be informed of and agree to comply with the Organization's information security policies relevant to their scope of services.

### Third-Party Access Control

**Access Terms and Conditions** — All third-party access to the Organization's information systems SHALL be governed by documented terms and conditions specifying security requirements, acceptable use, and liability provisions.

**Access Approval** — Third-party access SHALL require formal approval. Unique user IDs SHALL be assigned to all third-party personnel accessing the Organization's systems.

### Information Exchange

**Non-Disclosure Agreements** — Non-disclosure agreements SHALL be executed with all third parties before sharing confidential information.

**Information Handling at Contract Termination** — Upon contract termination, all Organization data in the possession of the third party SHALL be returned or securely destroyed, with certification of destruction provided.

### Third-Party Contracts

**Control Measures** — Third-party contracts SHALL include provisions addressing information security requirements, incident notification, audit rights, data handling obligations, and breach liability.

**Contract Approvals** — Third-party contracts involving access to confidential information SHALL be reviewed and approved by appropriate management and legal counsel.

**Reporting Security Violations** — Third parties SHALL be contractually obligated to promptly report any security violations or suspected incidents involving the Organization's data.

### Personnel Security

**Background Checks** — The Organization SHOULD require background checks for third-party personnel who will have access to confidential information or critical systems, commensurate with the sensitivity of the access granted.

### Software Procurement

**Third-Party Software Development** — When engaging third parties for software development, the Organization SHALL require adherence to its secure development standards and conduct security testing before deployment.

### Assessment, Monitoring, and Audits

**Ongoing Monitoring** — The Organization SHALL periodically monitor and review third-party service delivery to verify compliance with contractual security requirements. Third parties SHALL provide timely notice of any business or technical changes that may affect the Organization's security posture.

### Contingency Plans

**Service Provider Contingency** — Critical third-party service providers SHALL maintain contingency and disaster recovery plans commensurate with the criticality of services provided.

**Continuity Service Level Agreements** — Service level agreements with third parties SHALL include continuity and recovery commitments aligned with the Organization's business continuity requirements.

**Contract Failure Remedies** — Contracts SHALL include provisions for remedies and escalation procedures in the event of service failure or security breach.

## Definitions

- **Third Party**: Any non-employee who is contractually bound to provide some form of service to the Organization
- **Vendor Risk Assessment**: The process of evaluating a third-party vendor's security controls, compliance posture, and potential impact on the Organization's information security
- **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)**: A legal contract establishing confidential relationship between parties
