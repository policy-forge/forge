# Information Classification Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the requirements for assigning and maintaining classification settings for all information assets on the Organization's computer and communications systems.

## Scope

This policy applies to all computer systems and facilities of the Organization, with a target audience of all employees and third parties who handle information assets.

## Policy

### Asset Ownership

**Information Ownership** — All production information possessed by or used by a particular organizational unit SHALL have a designated Information Owner who is responsible for determining appropriate sensitivity classifications and criticality ratings, making decisions about who can access the information, and ensuring that appropriate controls are utilized in the storage, handling, distribution, and regular usage of information. Owners SHALL know the identity of the Custodians and users for the information types entrusted to their care.

**Information Custodian** — Each significant type of production information SHALL have a designated Custodian who will properly protect information in keeping with the designated Information Owner's access control, data sensitivity, and data criticality instructions.

**Information Systems Department** — With the exception of operational computer and network information, the Information Systems Department SHALL NOT be the Owner of any production business information.

### Asset Classification

**Three-Category Data Classification** — All data, including information entrusted from third parties, SHALL be classified into the following three sensitivity classifications: CONFIDENTIAL, INTERNAL, and PUBLIC. Distinct handling, labeling, and review procedures SHALL be established for each classification.

**Confidential** — This classification applies to highly sensitive business information intended for use only within the Organization. Unauthorized disclosure could adversely impact the Organization, its customers, suppliers, business partners, or employees. Personally identifiable information and intellectual property are included in this classification.

**Internal** — This information SHALL be disclosed only to third parties if a confidentiality agreement has been signed. Disclosure is not expected to cause serious harm, and access is provided freely to all internal workers via the organization's intranet. This is the default classification for any information not specifically designated.

**Public** — This classification applies to information approved by management for release to the public. There is no such thing as unauthorized disclosure of this information, and it may be disseminated without potential harm.

**Default Classification** — Information without a label is by default classified as Internal Use Only.

### Asset Labeling

**Assigning Classification Labels** — For all existing production information types, the Information Owner is responsible for choosing an appropriate data classification label to be used by all workers who create, compile, alter, or procure production information.

**Multiple Classification Labeling** — When information of various sensitivity classifications is combined, the resulting collection SHALL be classified at the most restricted level found anywhere in the sources.

## Definitions

- **Confidential Information**: Highly sensitive business information intended for internal use only, whose unauthorized disclosure could adversely impact the Organization
- **Information Owner**: The individual responsible for determining classification, access decisions, and appropriate controls for a set of information assets
- **Information Custodian**: Personnel in physical or logical possession of information who implement controls defined by the Owner
