# Crisis Communication Policy

## Purpose

This policy provides a framework for comprehensive and orderly internal and external communications before, during, and following a crisis event, ensuring consistent messaging, stakeholder management, and reputational protection.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees and contractors. It covers communication activities related to any crisis event that may impact the Organization's operations, reputation, or stakeholders.

## Policy

### When to Invoke the Crisis Communication Plan

A crisis communication plan SHALL be invoked when any of the following conditions apply:

1. The incident has the potential to harm the Organization from a competitive, reputational, or financial perspective
2. Internal or external stakeholders could be negatively impacted
3. Lack of proactive communication could be embarrassing, harmful, or could exacerbate the problem
4. Different stakeholders require uniquely crafted communications
5. Ongoing updates over a period of time are anticipated
6. There are unknowns that could escalate or de-escalate the severity

### Incident Lead

**Designation** — An Incident Lead SHALL be designated for each crisis event. The Incident Lead is responsible for managing remediation coordination, communicating updates to internal stakeholders, and serving as the single point of contact for information relating to the incident.

**Plan Maintenance** — The Incident Lead SHALL keep the crisis communication plan current and notify all internal stakeholders when updates are made.

### Situation Assessment

**Incident Description** — The Incident Lead SHALL document a complete summary of the incident, including an assessment of its severity and risks to stakeholders.

**Risk Categories** — The assessment SHALL address applicable risk categories including general security incidents (data loss, PII misuse, denial of service, ransomware) and product security incidents (native vulnerabilities, malware in components, third-party vulnerabilities).

**Stakeholder Impact** — Current and potential impacts SHALL be documented for each stakeholder group, including customers, prospects, employees, the board, and other relevant parties.

### Severity Classification

- **Critical (5)**: Significant reputational, financial, or security risk already occurring; immediate remediation critical
- **High (4)**: Significant risk imminent within six months; immediate remediation critical
- **Medium (3)**: Risk could occur but may not be significant within 6-12 months; planned remediation
- **Low (2)**: Some risk possible; incident monitored for potential escalation
- **Minimal (1)**: No significant risk anticipated; no remediation necessary

### Communications Approval

**Approval Requirement** — All communications under this plan SHALL be reviewed and approved by designated stakeholders including executive leadership, legal counsel, and corporate communications prior to dissemination.

**Approval Authority** — The Organization SHALL maintain a list of individuals authorized to approve crisis communications.

### Internal Communications

**Internal Stakeholder Groups** — The following groups SHALL be considered for internal communication as appropriate: senior leadership, cybersecurity committee, board of directors, legal, employees, human resources, corporate communications, technical support, customer success, and marketing.

**Messaging** — For each internal stakeholder group, approved talking points SHALL be developed that address the incident specifics relevant to that group's role and need to know.

**Internal Communicator** — The Incident Lead SHALL be responsible for all internal stakeholder communications unless otherwise designated.

### External Communications

**Objectives** — External communications SHALL aim to: engage subject matter experts for incident triage, provide forthright and comprehensive information to key stakeholders, minimize reputational damage and misinformation, and apply an appropriately measured strategy for information disclosure.

**External Stakeholder Groups** — The following groups SHALL be considered for external communication as appropriate: law enforcement, forensic investigators, regulatory authorities, affected customers, cyber insurance providers, and public relations or media contacts.

**Approved Messaging** — Approved talking points SHALL be developed for each external stakeholder group. Only designated external spokespeople are authorized to communicate about the incident.

### Reactive Communications

**Authorized Spokespeople** — Only designated external spokespeople SHALL communicate with external stakeholders about the incident.

**New Communications** — When inquiries arise that are not covered by approved talking points or FAQs, the spokesperson SHALL defer to the Incident Lead, who will formulate a proposed response for approval before dissemination.

**FAQs** — Where the incident is likely to generate varied questions from stakeholders, an approved FAQ document SHALL be developed.

### Social Media

**Social Media Response** — If social media communications are warranted, messaging SHALL be prepared and approved through the same approval process as other external communications.

### Ongoing Communications

**Communication Vehicles** — The Organization SHALL identify all appropriate communication channels, which may include email, meetings, internal collaboration platforms, customer communiques, press releases, and blog posts.

**Plan Storage** — Finalized crisis communication plans SHALL be stored in a centralized, accessible location so all stakeholders can access the most current version.

### Plan Ownership and Review

**Plan Review** — The crisis communication plan SHALL be reviewed at least annually and updated following any significant incident.

**Document Classification** — Crisis communication plans are classified as Internal and SHALL NOT be disclosed to unauthorized parties.

## Definitions

- **Crisis Event**: An incident or situation with the potential to cause significant harm to the Organization's operations, reputation, or stakeholders
- **Incident Lead**: The designated individual responsible for coordinating the crisis response and communications
- **External Spokesperson**: An individual authorized to communicate with external stakeholders regarding the crisis
