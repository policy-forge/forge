# Information Security Program Policy

## Purpose

This policy establishes the minimum requirements and responsibilities for protecting the Organization's information assets, preventing misuse and loss, establishing the basis for audits and self-assessments, and preserving management options and legal remedies in the event of asset loss or misuse.

## Scope

This policy applies to all computer systems and facilities of the Organization, including those managed by third parties. It applies to all employees, partners, and third parties with access to information assets.

## Policy

### Programs

**Information Security Program** — The Organization SHALL implement a comprehensive, written information security program that secures information assets in a manner commensurate with each asset's value as established by risk assessment and mitigation measures.

**Information Privacy Program** — The Organization SHALL implement a comprehensive, written information privacy program that secures employee and customer personally identifiable information (PII) against unauthorized use or disclosure.

**ISMS Objectives** — The Organization SHALL define clear information security objectives and establish a system of monitoring and measurement to record progress against targets.

### Policy and Procedures Requirements

Policies SHALL be implemented to assure the security, reliability, integrity, and availability of information assets. Procedures SHALL enforce these policies. Security and policy requirements SHALL be identified and documented during the early stages of all new programs and initiatives.

### Policy Sanctions

The Organization SHALL implement sanctions against employees and third parties who violate written information security policies.

### Policy Exceptions

The Organization SHALL maintain a documented process for tracking and approving exceptions to information security policies. All employees responsible for information security SHOULD submit written requests for exceptions. Approved exceptions SHALL be reviewed annually.

### Policy Distribution

Management SHALL publish written information security policies and make them available to all employees and relevant external parties. All employees and contractors SHALL review and acknowledge acceptance of applicable policies at least annually.

### Policy Review

All written information security policy documents SHALL be reviewed annually by a team consisting, at minimum, of members from the Information Security and Information Technology teams.

### Responsibility Assignment

Management SHALL allocate sufficient resources and staff to adequately address information systems security. The Organization SHALL define specific job roles for effective implementation of the security program. An individual SHALL be designated as Chief Information Security Officer (CISO). Management SHALL clearly assign and document accountability for every internal control.

### Program Reporting

Annual reports SHALL be submitted to management including: program status, updated risk assessment, management decisions on risk mitigation and residual risk acceptance, service provider oversight activities, results of key control testing, and management response to identified deficiencies.

### Program Review and Maintenance

The security program SHALL be updated and re-approved annually or whenever there is a material change in the Organization or infrastructure. Program updates SHALL incorporate results from risk assessments.

### Security in Project Management

Whenever a new system or project involves confidential information, the Information Security Team SHOULD ensure security considerations are part of the project management and review process. Security requirements SHALL consider authentication, access rights, user education, asset requirements, mandatory controls, and product testing.

### Security Program Compliance

The Organization SHALL include an analysis and definition of all relevant statutory, regulatory, and contractual requirements. Independent external reviews of information systems security SHALL be periodically obtained. An external penetration test SHALL be performed annually or following significant infrastructure changes.
