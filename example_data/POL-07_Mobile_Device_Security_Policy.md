# Mobile Device Security Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the minimum requirements for the protection of information stored, processed, or transported on mobile devices, whether Organization-owned or personal devices accessing corporate data.

## Scope

This policy applies to all mobile devices, including Organization-owned mobile devices and applications installed on employees' personal mobile devices that access corporate data.

## Policy

### Configuration and Issuing of Mobile Devices

**Organization-Owned Mobile Devices** — Managers SHALL request a mobile device from the IT department for an employee. The device SHALL be issued by the IT department and used solely for business purposes.

**Mobile Device Management** — Organization-owned mobile devices SHALL be controlled by a mobile device management (MDM) system. Devices SHALL be subject to compliance rules on security features, enforced by the IT department using MDM software.

**Configuration and Software Changes** — On Organization-owned mobile devices, employees SHOULD keep the operating system and applications up to date. Software SHALL only be installed from official platform-approved sources.

**Data Protection** — Mobile devices SHALL enforce data storage encryption.

**Applications** — Applications SHALL only be installed from official platform-approved sources and SHOULD be kept up to date.

### Personal Mobile Devices

**Authorized Application Access** — Employees SHOULD only access corporate information from personal mobile devices through authorized applications, limited to email, instant messaging, voice and video calling, and document viewing through approved corporate applications.

**Data Storage Prohibition** — Storage of corporate data outside authorized applications is prohibited on personal devices. To access additional data and services, employees SHOULD use an Organization-managed device.

**Personal Accounts** — No corporate data SHALL be sent through personal accounts. If an employee suspects that corporate data has been sent from a personal email account, they SHOULD notify IT Security immediately.

### Physical Security Protection

**Personnel Responsibility** — All Organization-owned mobile devices and personal devices containing corporate data SHALL be protected from physical threats such as loss due to theft or vandalism. Employees SHOULD NOT leave mobile devices unattended where they can be easily seen or stolen.

### Authentication and Network Access

**Automatic Wireless Network Connections** — All Organization-owned mobile devices SHALL be configured so that automatic network connection is disabled and SHALL only connect to secure networks.

**Strong Authentication Required** — All mobile devices used to access corporate data SHOULD have strong authentication enabled.

### Lost or Stolen Mobile Devices

**Immediate Reporting** — All employees SHOULD immediately report lost or stolen Organization-owned mobile devices, or personal devices containing corporate data, to both their immediate supervisor and the IT department.

**Unauthorized Access** — If an employee suspects unauthorized access to an Organization-owned mobile device or corporate information on a personal device, they SHOULD immediately report this to both their immediate supervisor and the IT department.

## Definitions

- **Mobile Device**: A portable computing device including, but not limited to, smartphones and tablets; excludes mobile computing devices such as laptops
- **Mobile Device Management (MDM)**: Software used to manage, monitor, and secure mobile devices deployed across the Organization
