# Information Security Organization Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the specific information security roles, responsibilities, and organizational structure for implementing the Organization's information security program.

## Scope

This policy applies to all computer systems and facilities of the Organization, with a target audience of Information Technology employees and partners.

## Policy

### Management Commitment to Information Security

Management SHALL provide clear direction and visible support for security initiatives.

**Information Security Council** — The Organization SHALL create an information security council composed of appropriate stakeholders. The council SHALL meet regularly to review the status of information security, approve and review security projects, approve new or modified policies, and perform other high-level security management activities.

**Policy-Driven Architecture** — The Organization SHALL employ a policy-driven information systems security architecture approach that is coordinated and managed by the Information Security Program Team and integrated into the risk management process.

### Information Security Planning

Management SHALL allocate sufficient resources and staff attention to adequately address information systems security.

### Information Security Responsibility Assignment

**Defining Specific Security Roles** — The Organization SHALL define specific job roles required for effective implementation of the security program. Each role SHALL include a description of security-related duties performed by team members.

**Department Responsibilities** — The Information Security Program Team SHALL define the specific duties of each department (human resources, audit, IT) required to support security efforts.

**Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)** — The Organization SHALL explicitly designate at least one individual responsible for the information security function, titled CISO. The CISO SHALL provide executive-level reporting on the state of information security.

### Department Responsibilities

- **Information Security Team**: Centralized guidance, direction, and authority for security activities. Responsible for establishing and maintaining organization-wide security policies, standards, guidelines, and procedures.
- **Incident Response Team**: Investigations of system intrusions and security incidents.
- **Internal Audit Team**: Compliance checking to ensure organizational units operate consistently with security requirements.
- **Human Resources**: Disciplinary matters resulting from violations of security requirements, handled with local managers.

### Contact with Special Interest Groups

Information security professionals SHOULD maintain memberships with security forums and professional associations. Each security staff member SHOULD have an annual technical training and professional development plan.

### Employee Information Security Roles

The Organization defines three categories of information security responsibilities:

- **Owner**: Department managers or delegates responsible for acquisition, development, and maintenance of production applications. Owners designate confidentiality classifications, define user access, and approve information usage.
- **Custodian**: Personnel in physical or logical possession of information. Custodians implement access controls, make backups, and maintain security measures defined by Owners.
- **User**: Responsible for familiarizing themselves with and complying with all security policies and procedures.

The Information Security Team SHALL provide Owners with training, reference material, and consulting assistance for making classification and access decisions.
