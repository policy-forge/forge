# Security Awareness and Training Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the information security-related requirements for training all personnel with access to sensitive information, ensuring a culture of security awareness throughout the Organization.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees of the Organization.

## Policy

### Management Responsibilities

**Minimum Training Requirements** — The Information Security Department SHALL specify a minimum set of training and awareness-raising requirements for all workers, for all workers with access to information systems, and for all workers in positions of trust. Managers SHOULD additionally ensure that their direct reports have sufficient training and technical skills to securely operate the information systems to which they have been granted access.

**Security Training Time** — Management SHALL allocate sufficient on-the-job time for workers to acquaint themselves with security policies, procedures, and related ways of doing business.

**Training Materials** — The Information Security Department SHALL provide refresher courses and other materials to regularly remind workers about their obligations and responsibilities with respect to information security.

### Security Policy Awareness

**Policy Distribution** — All new workers SHALL be given access to information security policies and made aware that compliance with these policies is a condition of continued employment.

**Policy Compliance Agreement** — Every worker SHALL understand and agree to perform work according to the Organization's information security policies and procedures. Personnel SHALL acknowledge annually, either in writing or electronically, their understanding of policies related to information security.

**Policy Change Notification** — All workers SHALL receive notice of major changes in information security policies that pertain to them, including how these changes may affect them and how to obtain additional information.

**Security Violations and Reporting** — Users SHALL be clearly informed about actions that constitute security violations, that all such violations will be logged, and how to properly report possible security incidents.

### Security Awareness and Training

**Initial Training** — Every worker SHALL be assigned information security awareness training when they begin employment.

**Annual Training** — Employees SHALL be assigned and SHOULD complete an information security training course on an annual basis. Completing this course SHALL require passing a test.

**Training Materials** — All workers SHALL be provided with sufficient training and supporting reference material to permit them to properly protect information resources.

**Training Records** — Management SHALL maintain a listing of the training provided to and completed by all users of the Organization's information assets.

**Phishing Awareness** — The security team SHALL periodically conduct phishing awareness campaigns to test phishing resiliency.

### Security Awareness Reminders

**Ongoing Awareness** — All employees SHALL be regularly exposed to additional information security awareness topics that may be relevant to their roles.

## Definitions

- **Security Awareness Training**: Formal education designed to inform employees about information security threats, policies, and best practices
- **Phishing**: A social engineering technique used to deceive individuals into revealing sensitive information through fraudulent communications
