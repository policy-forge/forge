# IT Risk Assessment Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the requirements for assessing risk related to the Organization's information assets and systems, establishing a consistent methodology for identifying, evaluating, and treating information security risks.

## Scope

This policy applies to all computer systems, facilities, and information assets of the Organization, including those managed by third parties. It applies to all employees, partners, and third parties with access to information assets.

## Policy

### IT Risk Management Process

**Enterprise Security Risk Assessment** — Each year, Information Technology SHALL conduct or manage an independent organization-wide security risk assessment. The resulting report SHALL include a detailed description of information security risks currently facing the Organization and specific recommendations for prevention or mitigation.

**Risk Assessment Methodology** — The Organization SHALL adopt and document a formal risk assessment process. This process MUST include:

- Assessment of inherent risks, including likelihood and impact of loss of confidentiality, integrity, and availability
- Identification of residual risks after implementation of controls relative to inherent risk
- Definition of risk tolerance levels based on business requirements
- Application of mitigation efforts to residual risks that exceed tolerance levels

### Risk Treatment

**Material Information Security Risks** — For every material or significant information security risk identified, whether through formal risk assessment or other means, management SHALL decide the degree to which the Organization will:

- Tolerate and accept the risk
- Seek external insurance coverage
- Adjust controls to reduce the risk level to an acceptable cost of conducting business

These decisions SHALL be documented.

**Internal Controls Responsibility** — Every information security-related control SHALL be specifically assigned to a responsible individual or team.

### Information Systems Risk Management

**Information Security Impact Analysis** — Whenever confidential information is used in new or substantially different ways, a risk assessment of potential security-related impacts SHOULD be performed before deployment.

### Insurance

**Insurance Coverage** — The Organization SHOULD maintain insurance commensurate with residual risks identified from corporate risk assessments or business impact analyses that pose potential for financial loss or other disastrous consequences.

**Insurance Review** — An annual insurance review SHALL be conducted to ensure coverage levels and types are commercially reasonable and consistent with legal, management, and board requirements.

## Definitions

- **Risk**: The result of a threat acting on a vulnerability, expressed as a product of likelihood and severity of impact
- **Risk Assessment**: The determination of quantitative or qualitative value of risk related to a recognized threat, producing a report showing assets, vulnerabilities, likelihood of damage, and defensive measures
- **Residual Risk**: The risk that remains after a control is applied to an identified risk
- **Risk Tolerance**: The defined threshold of acceptable risk based on business requirements
- **Risk Mitigation**: The process of prioritizing, implementing, and maintaining risk-reducing measures
