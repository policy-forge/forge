# Incident Response Policy

## Purpose

This policy provides a well-defined and organized approach for handling actual or potential threats to the Organization's business or customer information, whether maintained electronically or in any other format.

## Scope

This policy addresses privacy and security incidents involving all organizational data, including data under the control or responsibility of a vendor or third party.

## Policy

### Goals of Incident Response

In the event of a privacy or security incident, the Incident Response Team SHALL:

1. Investigate the incident internally, in cooperation with law enforcement if necessary
2. Mitigate potential harm to affected parties
3. Minimize adverse impact in an ethically and legally appropriate manner
4. Appropriately communicate the incident to affected parties, regulatory agencies, and staff
5. Provide guidance in the development of corrective actions
6. Conduct post-incident reviews to minimize future incidents

### Defining a Security Incident

**Privacy Incident** — Any attempt at or occurrence of unauthorized acquisition, exposure, disclosure, use, modification, or destruction of sensitive data that compromises the security, confidentiality, or integrity of business information or individually identifiable information.

**Security Incident** — Any known or suspected event or condition which may put the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of sensitive data at risk.

### Incident Response Team

The Incident Response Team SHALL include appropriate representatives from: the CISO/CIO office, Legal, Senior Leadership, Security management, Information Technology, Engineering, Public Relations, and any engaged third-party incident response vendors.

### Incident Timeline

**Initial Response** — Begins immediately upon discovery or report. Includes discovery and reporting, determination that an incident has occurred, management involvement, containment and prevention, legal involvement, documentation, and escalation.

**Continuing Response** — Includes analysis and planning, investigation, forensics and evidence preservation, mitigation and correction, notification, incident closure, and reporting.

### Discovery and Reporting

**Reporting Obligation** — All employees SHALL report suspected security incidents immediately to the Information Security Department.

**Initial Assessment** — Upon receiving a report, the Information Security Department SHALL perform an initial assessment to determine whether a security incident has occurred and its potential severity.

### Containment and Prevention

**Immediate Containment** — Upon confirmation of an incident, the Incident Response Team SHALL take immediate steps to contain the breach and prevent further unauthorized access or data loss.

**Evidence Preservation** — Containment actions SHALL preserve evidence for potential forensic investigation and legal proceedings.

### Legal Involvement

**Legal Consultation** — Legal counsel SHALL be consulted early in the incident response process.

**Cyber Insurance** — If the Organization maintains cyber insurance, the provider SHALL be notified per policy terms.

### Investigation and Forensics

**Investigation** — The Incident Response Team SHALL conduct a thorough investigation to determine the scope, cause, and impact of the incident.

**Chain of Custody** — Evidence SHALL be collected and preserved following a documented chain of custody procedure.

**Forensic Analysis** — Where required, forensic analysis SHALL be performed by qualified internal staff or a trusted third party.

### Notification

**Regulatory Notification** — Notification to regulatory authorities SHALL be made within timeframes required by applicable laws.

**Individual Notification** — Affected individuals SHALL be notified per applicable breach notification laws.

**Customer Notification** — Customers SHALL be notified per contractual obligations.

**Law Enforcement** — Law enforcement SHALL be notified when a cybercrime is suspected.

### Severity Levels

- **Critical (5)**: Significant risk already occurring; immediate remediation necessary
- **High (4)**: Significant risk imminent within six months; immediate remediation necessary
- **Medium (3)**: Risk could occur but is not significant within 6-12 months; planned remediation
- **Low (2)**: Some risk possible; incident monitored for escalation
- **Minimal (1)**: No significant risk anticipated; no remediation necessary

### Post-Incident Activities

**Incident Closure** — The incident case file SHALL be formally closed when all response activities are complete and lessons learned are documented.

**Post-Incident Review** — A review SHALL be conducted to evaluate response effectiveness and identify improvements.

**Final Report** — A final incident report SHALL summarize the incident, response actions, impact, and prevention recommendations.

## Definitions

- **Privacy Incident**: Unauthorized acquisition, exposure, disclosure, use, modification, or destruction of personally identifiable information
- **Security Incident**: A known or suspected event that may put confidentiality, integrity, or availability of sensitive data at risk
- **Chain of Custody**: Documented chronological record of the handling, transfer, and disposition of evidence
