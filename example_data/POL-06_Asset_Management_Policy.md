# Asset Management Policy

## Purpose

This policy establishes the minimum requirements and responsibilities for the protection of the Organization's equipment and media assets throughout the asset lifecycle, from procurement to disposal.

## Scope

This policy applies to all computer systems and facilities of the Organization, including those managed by third parties. It applies to all employees, partners, and third parties with access to information assets.

## Policy

### Asset Procurement

**Hardware and Software Procurement** — All hardware and software SHALL be procured through the Purchasing Department according to IT compatibility standards.

**Mission-Critical Vendors** — All mission-critical hardware, software, and services SHALL be purchased, rented, leased, or otherwise obtained from trusted and well-established vendors capable of providing maintenance services and warranties.

### Asset Inventory

**Corporate Inventory** — The Information Technology department SHALL maintain an inventory of corporate information systems hardware and software. Periodic reviews SHALL be completed to ensure that the inventory remains accurate and complete.

**Inventory Contents** — Every asset recorded in the asset inventory SHALL include, at minimum: asset name, asset owner, and asset location.

**Equipment Tags** — All computer and communications equipment SHALL have a unique identifier attached to enable efficient and regular physical inventories.

**Inventory Updates** — The IT department SHALL update inventory records when changes such as asset location, name, ownership, or status occur.

### Ownership

**Asset Ownership** — All production information assets possessed by or used by the Organization SHOULD have a designated owner with ownership responsibilities clearly documented.

### Equipment Authorization

**Approved Security Configuration** — All computer and communication equipment issued to users SHALL be configured according to standards approved by the Information Security Department. Assets SHALL be monitored and maintained based on established security requirements, including installation of only approved images during initial configuration, restrictions on local administrator privileges, periodic patch updates, and operating system hardening to provide only necessary ports, services, and protocols.

**Software Installation** — Technical controls SHALL be implemented to restrict the installation of software on operational systems. Requirements SHALL govern the types of software installations permitted and prohibited on information resources.

### Property Handling

**Use of Assets** — Assets SHALL be used in accordance with the Acceptable Use Policy.

**Storage of Assets** — IT assets SHALL be stored in accordance with manufacturers' specifications.

**Approval for Removal** — Computers and peripherals assigned to a specific employee are pre-approved for removal from offices for remote working use. Before any multi-user servers, removable media, or other equipment is removed from or between offices or data centers, a change control ticket MUST be created and approval obtained from Information Technology management.

**Third-Party Conveyance** — Before equipment of any type is sold, disposed of, recycled, or otherwise conveyed to a third party, approval SHALL first be obtained from Information Technology management.

**Return of Mobile Devices** — All Organization-issued mobile devices, including laptops and cell phones, SHALL be returned when no longer in use by employees or contractors.

**Incident Reporting** — Loss, theft, or other information security incidents related to assets SHALL be reported immediately per the Incident Response Policy.

### Disposal of Computer Equipment

**Equipment Disposal** — All data storage devices SHALL be destroyed, or the data sanitized using certified third-party vendors, and certificates of destruction SHALL be obtained. Endpoints MUST be encrypted before being sent to a third party for destruction. All assets MUST be fully secured until destruction.

**Decommissioned Equipment Inventory** — The Information Technology Department SHALL maintain an inventory of all corporate computer and network equipment that has been taken out of commission.

## Definitions

- **Information Asset**: Any data in any form, and the equipment used to manage, process, or store that data, which is used in the course of executing business
- **Decommissioned Equipment**: Hardware that has been permanently removed from operational use and is pending disposal or recycling
