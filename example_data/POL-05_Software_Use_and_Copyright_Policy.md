# Software Use and Copyright Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the requirements for compliance with software license agreements and related copyrights on all the Organization's computer and communications systems. The Organization is licensed to use computer software from a variety of vendors and other sources and does not own this software or its related documentation unless specifically authorized.

## Scope

This policy applies to all users and third parties who use proprietary or copyrighted software on the Organization's systems.

## Policy

### Copyright Protection

All users MUST comply with federal copyright laws, nondisclosure agreements, and vendor licensing agreements governing the installation, use, and distribution of purchased software. All software SHALL be used in accordance with the developer's license agreements and the Organization's code of ethics.

### Software Procurement

All software used on Organization-owned computers SHALL be purchased through appropriate procurement procedures and from approved vendors. Personnel SHOULD consult their supervisor or IT management for proper procurement procedures before acquiring new software.

### Acceptable Use and Ownership

**Remote Worker Usage** — When a user needs to use software for business purposes at home, the Organization SHALL either confirm that the license allows home use or purchase an additional copy or license.

**Software Ownership** — Any additional copies of software issued for home use are the property of the Organization.

**Personal Software Installation** — Users MUST NOT install personal software onto the Organization's computer systems.

**Authorized Use Only** — Users SHALL use software only according to manufacturer license agreements and this policy. The Organization licenses software from outside companies but does not own the copyright to that software.

**Unauthorized Duplication** — Users MUST NOT make unauthorized copies of any copyrighted software or intellectual property. Unauthorized duplication of copyrighted computer software violates the law and is contrary to corporate standards of conduct. The Organization prohibits all forms of software piracy.

### Software Auditing

The Organization reserves the right to audit the software contents of any Organization-owned device to ensure proper usage and licensing of all applications. Unlicensed or unauthorized software discovered during audits may be removed without notice.

## Definitions

- **Authorized Software**: Software that has been purchased, licensed, and approved for use on the Organization's systems through proper procurement channels
- **Software Piracy**: The unauthorized copying, distribution, or use of copyrighted software, which is a violation of international copyright law
